Welcome to my website! If you're on this page you're probably hoping to learn a bit more about me. I'll note that this is in-progress so expect to see incremental updates. I also apologize for the redundancy in information between pages.

I'm [Chamorro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamorro_people), and was born and raised on the island of [Saipan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saipan%2C_Northern_Mariana_Islands). I feel incredibly lucky to have grown up in such a beautiful culture.

I recently graduated from UC Berkeley, where I studied Computer Science and worked eight semesters as a teaching assistant [Data 8](https://data8.org/su26/) and [Data 6](https://data6.org/fa25/). My role as a TA was one of the most formative parts of my time as an undergrad, as I discovered my love for teaching programming.

I also organize as a proud member of [UAW Local 4811](https://www.uaw4811.org/), the union of ~~48,000~~ [60,000 academic workers](https://rpsp-uaw.org/2026/05/20/60000-strong-rpsps-and-ssaps-join-uaw-local-4811/) across 11 campuses at the University of California. During undergrad, I organized with fellow [EECS and Data Science Workers](https://eecsdsstaff.org/) and was part of the bargaining team for our [side letter](https://eecsdsstaff.org/know-your-rights/our-local-agreement/). It was because of our organizing that we had strong workplace protections to maintain high quality of instruction.

Now, I'm a first-year M.S. candidate at the [UCSC Baskin School of Engineering](https://engineering.ucsc.edu/), and I'm a lead TA for CSE 20: Beginning Programming in Python. Through my role, I'm helping to update our curriculum for the A.I. age, with a focus on computational literacy.
